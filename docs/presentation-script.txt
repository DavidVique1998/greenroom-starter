PRESENTATION SCRIPT — Settlement Signal Integrity (6 min)
==========================================================


[0:00 – 0:45] CONTEXT — WHERE THIS COMES FROM
(MD open, Context section visible)

"I'll start with context because the framing matters. Pri Iyer wrote in her Q4 memo: 'We are winning on completeness and losing on craft.' The sharpest edge of that gap is settlements — 82% of Greenroom customers run settlements in spreadsheets, not in the product. The Crescent is in that 82%.

I talked to Mariana Reyes, the lead booker. She described her Friday night ritual: open Greenroom for deal terms, open Google Sheets for the math, copy-paste between them at 2am. Greenroom isn't in that room. The trust between the venue and the artist is being built somewhere else."


[0:45 – 1:30] THE PROBLEM
(scroll to The Problem section)

"This case study doesn't fix Vs deals — that's a full sprint on its own. It fixes something more immediate: Mariana has settlements stuck in 'disputed' status that the artist team already approved. The data is right there: signoffs like 'Looks good — TM', thumbs up, 'ok wire monday', 'OK. Good night.' — sitting next to status = disputed.

Four settlements in this state right now, each one blocking a payment for no reason. From Mariana's perspective this is invisible friction — she has to remember to go back, find the show, re-read the thread, and manually push it forward. And the downstream costs are real: one stale status in March 2025 — the Coastal Spell dispute — started exactly like this and cost the venue $720 plus hours of Marcus and Mariana's time."


[1:30 – 2:00] WHY THIS SLICE
(scroll to Why This Slice, table visible)

"I had four options on the table. Vs-deal math repair is the highest-impact work but also the highest risk — shipping bad math is worse than no math. Real-time dispute prediction is interesting but needs an ML pipeline I can't prototype without infra. Agent comm drafting is something Mariana asked for directly in her interview — but a draft message is only trustworthy if the underlying status is accurate. Signal integrity unblocks that one.

Signal integrity wins because it's the smallest intervention with the clearest outcome. Every resolved flagged settlement is a payment unblocked. The value is in dollars and time, not engagement metrics."


[2:00 – 3:00] WHAT I BUILT — THE CLASSIFIER AND THE FLOW
(scroll to What I Built, mermaid diagram visible)

"Three surfaces. Everything flows through a signal classifier — keyword matching on the signoff text. If the text scores positive, the system checks internal notes. Clean notes: rose flag, clear mismatch, payment is blocked for no reason. Conflicting notes: amber flag, positive signoff but a later note raised a question. Both surfaces — the audit queue and the inline banner on the settlement page — give Mariana one click to resolve.

Why keyword matching and not an LLM? Because this runs with zero infrastructure, no API key, no latency dependency. The false-positive case is handled by design — Mariana reads every card and decides. No automated action happens without her."

[5:15 – 5:40] WHAT WAS CUT AND WHY

"A few deliberate cuts. No bulk resolve — each one is real money, Mariana should read every card. No LLM classification in V1 — keyword matching handles every case in the current data and ships with zero dependencies. Flagged-since timestamp and Slack notifications are next — they're high value but need a bit more schema work and a notification infra decision."


[5:40 – 6:00] WHAT SHIPS NEXT + CLOSE

And the larger unlock — Vs-deal math repair. Signal integrity is the foundation. The payment numbers need to be right before we automate anything further downstream. That's what closes the gap on Pri's 82%."
